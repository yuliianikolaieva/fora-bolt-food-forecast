Model assumptions

How the forecast is built: rollout schedule, store ramp-up curve, scenario per-store rate, and the order→GMV conversion.

Location rollout

  • July — 100 locations, launch from Jul 20 (~12 operating days, partial month)
  • August — 150 (+50)
  • September — 216 (+66)
  • October–December — 216 (full coverage)

Ramp-up to maturity

  • A new location does not deliver full volume immediately. Share of mature rate by months live: M1 30% · M2 55% · M3 75% · M4 88% · M5 95% · M6 100%.
  • Each cohort (Jul / Aug / Sep) ramps independently, so the blended orders/location/day rises over time.

Orders → GMV

  • Average order value (AOV) = €12.
  • GMV = orders × €12.
  • Sanity check: Bolt retail AOV in the source report ≈ €11.8.

Scenario per-store rate (at maturity)

  • Base: Bolt reaches 40% of Glovo Fora's per-store rate → 4.24 orders/location/day.
  • Optimistic: Bolt reaches 65% → 6.89 orders/location/day.
  • Anchored on Glovo Fora = 10.6 orders/store/day (mature).

Why 40% in the Base case

  • Chains active on both platforms give Bolt 5% (Kopiyka) → 12% (VARUS) → 35% (Rukavychka) of Glovo's per-store rate.
  • VARUS — the closest supermarket analog — reached 12% in its very first ramp month and is still climbing. Base assumes Fora matures to 40% of Glovo's rate.

Why 65% in the Optimistic case

  • Bolt retail is growing fast: +74% Jan→May (18.7K → 32.6K orders/month).
  • With active promo support and Fora's strong brand, the mature rate reaches 65% of Glovo (6.89 orders/location/day).

Key takeaways

H2 2026 summary

  • Base: ~98.5K orders / €1.18M GMV.
  • Optimistic: ~160.1K orders / €1.92M GMV.
  • July reflects only ~12 operating days (launch Jul 20).

December run-rate

  • 216 locations deliver 872–1,416 orders/day (4.04–6.56 per location).
  • For reference, Fora on Glovo runs at 10.6 orders/store/day.

Main risk driver — ramp speed

  • The forecast is most sensitive to how fast new locations mature and to the per-store rate relative to Glovo.
  • Dual-platform chains span a wide band (5% → 35% of Glovo), so launch promo support is critical.

Market headroom

  • Fora on Glovo does ~59.5K orders/month across 182 stores.
  • Even the Base case in December reaches ~45% of that volume — meaningful headroom remains for 2027.
