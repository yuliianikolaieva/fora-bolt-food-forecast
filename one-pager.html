Why we believe these numbers

  • Fora is already proven in Ukraine retail delivery: on Glovo it does ~59.5K orders/month across 182 stores (10.6 orders/store/day) — real May 2026 data.
  • Bolt converts a fraction of that per store at launch and ramps up. Comparable chains on both platforms run at 5–35% of Glovo's per-store rate; VARUS (closest supermarket analog) hit 12% in its first month and is still climbing.
  • Bolt retail is growing fast: +74% Jan→May 2026 — a supportive tailwind for a new launch.
  • Base = 40% of Glovo's per-store rate, Optimistic = 65%, each phased in over a 6-month store ramp (30%→100%).

How it's calculated

  • Mature rate per store: Glovo Fora 10.6 orders/store/day × scenario share → Base 4.24, Optimistic 6.89 orders/loc/day.
  • Ramp factor by months a store has been live: 30% · 55% · 75% · 88% · 95% · 100% (each cohort ramps separately).
  • Orders/loc/day (month) = mature rate × blended ramp factor across live cohorts.
  • Orders/month = orders/loc/day × locations × operating days (Jul = 12, others = full month).
  • GMV/month = orders/month × €12 (AOV).

Key risks & what they depend on

Ramp speed

  • New stores take ~6 months to mature. Slower onboarding pushes volume to 2027.

Per-store rate vs Glovo

  • Dual-platform chains span 5–35%. Landing point is driven by promo & visibility support at launch.

July is partial

  • Launch on Jul 20 → only ~12 operating days, so July contributes a small fraction of a full month.

Recommendation

The Fora launch is attractive and low-risk on the downside: even the conservative Base case delivers ~98.5K orders / €1.18M GMV in just five-and-a-half months, reaching ~45% of Fora's current Glovo volume by December — with clear headroom to grow into 2027. Upside to €1.92M is realistic with active promo support. Proceed with the rollout and prioritise launch-month marketing to land closer to the Optimistic per-store rate; treat ramp speed and per-store performance vs Glovo as the two metrics to track monthly.
